Privacy

How MEMRYA handles your recordings, transcripts and account data.

Last updated: pre-launch, private development.

This page will be revised before MEMRYA is generally available.

Contact: dev@memrya.co

MEMRYA is currently in private development, preparing its first paid release. This page describes how the product is built today. Final retention and legal policy is still being completed and will be published before public launch.

What MEMRYA processes

MEMRYA's core flow is recording or importing a conversation, then uploading, transcribing and analyzing it to produce a summary, evidence and grounded answers you can search later. Audio you record or import is the basis for everything else the product creates.

Where processing happens

Recording on your device is local. When you choose cloud processing, your audio and transcript may be sent securely to approved processing providers to perform transcription and analysis. This only happens after you choose processing — it is not automatic or hidden.

Storage and access

Cloud storage is private, and access is scoped to the account that owns the data. Tenant isolation — keeping each account's data separate from every other account — is treated as a product invariant, not an optional setting.

Secrets and keys

Provider and API secrets used to talk to processing providers live server-side. They are never embedded in the mobile client.

Encryption

We do not claim end-to-end encryption. Data is protected through private, owner-scoped cloud storage and server-side secret handling as described above, not through an end-to-end encryption guarantee.

Voice Identity

Voice Identity — recognizing a voice across conversations — is designed for explicit enrollment and local, on-device profile storage. It is still in validation and is not generally available. Weak or ambiguous matches are surfaced only as suggestions, never as guesses.

Languages

Spoken language and result language are handled separately in the product. Processing providers support a broad set of languages, but transcription quality is not equal across every one of them.

Retention

Final retention and legal policy is still being completed ahead of public launch. Specifics will be published here before MEMRYA is generally available.

Export and deletion

Account export and delete functions exist on the server side. Public-launch quality testing on those flows is still open. Until that testing is complete, you can request deletion by contacting dev@memrya.co.

No analytics or trackers

This website uses no analytics, cookies, trackers or third-party JavaScript, and sets no public API keys.

Questions

For any privacy question, contact dev@memrya.co.
