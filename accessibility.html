Accessibility

The accessibility commitments this build of MEMRYA actually makes.

SCOPE

This page covers the public website. It does not claim any formal certification.

CONTACT

dev@memrya.co

What this site does

  • Semantic HTML headings and landmark structure on every page, with one <h1> per page.
  • A visible skip-to-content link at the top of every page for keyboard users.
  • Visible keyboard focus states on links, buttons, form controls and the language switcher.
  • Support for prefers-reduced-motion ג€” reveal animations are disabled and all content is shown immediately for users who request reduced motion.
  • The FAQ on the homepage is built with native <details>/<summary> elements, so it is readable and operable without JavaScript.
  • True right-to-left layout for Hebrew and Arabic, including mirrored spacing and directional icons, not just mirrored text.
  • A color palette chosen to keep body text readable against the paper and stone backgrounds used throughout the site.

What we don't claim

We do not claim WCAG conformance or any other accessibility certification for this site. The list above describes the concrete choices made in this build, not a compliance guarantee, and it will keep evolving alongside the product.

Feedback

If something is hard to use with a keyboard, a screen reader, or under reduced motion, email dev@memrya.co and describe what happened ג€” we will look into it.
