Current status

Account export and delete functions exist on the server side. Public-launch quality assurance on those flows is still open, so we are not yet presenting a finished self-serve, in-app deletion flow. Email is the current, reliable way to request deletion.

Export and delete are real, working server-side functions — they simply have not yet completed the QA pass required before we expose them as a public, self-serve flow.

How to request deletion

Email dev@memrya.co from the address associated with your MEMRYA account and ask for your account and data to be deleted. Include any details that help us locate your account, such as the device or approximate sign-up date.

What happens next

We confirm the request and delete your account and associated data server-side. Because the automated, public-launch deletion path is still being validated, requests made today may be handled directly by the team rather than through a fully automated pipeline.

Data export

If you want a copy of your data before deletion, mention that in the same email and we will include it as part of handling your request.
