Why there are no numbers yet

Final App Store and RevenueCat subscription prices have not been set publicly. We are not going to guess or pre-announce numbers here — final pricing will be published once it is set, ahead of general launch.

What "processing minutes" means

Recording a conversation on your device is intended to stay free. When a conversation is sent for cloud processing — transcription and analysis — that usage is measured in processing minutes. The exact minute allowances and plans have not been finalized.

We never invent prices, discounts or plan names ahead of time. If a number isn't in dev@memrya.co's official communication or the App Store listing, treat it as not yet decided.
