What MEMRYA is

MEMRYA is an iPhone-first AI conversation memory app. Record or import a conversation and it moves through upload, transcription and analysis to become a Summary, Evidence and an Ask interface you can return to later, with export and share options when you need them.

Conversation memory is the core idea: not just a transcript, but the people, decisions and moments worth finding again.

What makes MEMRYA different

MEMRYA is built to preserve more than a transcript: speakers, decisions, evidence and context stay connected so a conversation can be searched and understood later.

Privacy and control

MEMRYA is designed with private, owner-scoped storage, server-side secrets and account-level tenant isolation. See the Security and Privacy pages for details.
