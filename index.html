FAQ

Questions worth answering upfront.

What does MEMRYA actually record and store?

MEMRYA turns a recorded or imported conversation into a transcript, summary, decisions, people and evidence you can search later. Cloud storage is private and access is owner-scoped.

Is Voice Identity available now?

Not yet. Voice Identity is designed for explicit enrollment and on-device profile storage, and it is still in validation. Weak or ambiguous matches are shown as suggestions, never guesses.

Which languages does MEMRYA support?

The interface is available in English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian, with true right-to-left layout for Arabic. The processing providers behind transcription cover a broader set of languages, though quality is not equal across every one of them.

Can I import from Zoom or Google Meet?

Zoom cloud-recording import architecture exists but is not yet publicly enabled. Google Meet is planned after Zoom, and Microsoft Teams later. Importing existing audio you already have works today.

How much will MEMRYA cost?

Recording on device is intended to stay free. Cloud processing uses processing minutes. Final App Store and subscription pricing has not been set publicly yet.

Is my data encrypted end-to-end?

We do not claim end-to-end encryption. Cloud storage is private and access-scoped to your account, provider secrets stay server-side, and tenant isolation is a product invariant.

How long is my data retained?

Final retention and legal policy is still being completed ahead of public launch. We will publish specifics before MEMRYA is generally available.

Can I delete my account and data?

Account export and delete functions exist on the server side. Public-launch quality testing on those flows is still open, so reach out to dev@memrya.co to request deletion today.
